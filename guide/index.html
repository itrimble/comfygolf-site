Comfy Golf Play the demo

How to play

One button, three presses. The caddie reads the shot; you time the swing.

The swing

  1. Read the shot. The caddie names the club and the line, and puts a red mark on the power meter where the ball reaches the pin. The same mark is on the swing arc.
  2. Press once to start. The marker climbs. Press again at the red mark to lock the power. More power goes further, with less room for error.
  3. Press on SNAP. The marker comes back. Press when it crosses the SNAP line: early pulls the ball left, late pushes it right. On the line it is straight.

On a phone, the 3-Click mode uses a tap zone for the same three presses. Slingshot is the alternative: pull back for power, slide sideways for curl, release to strike.

The caddie and the pin mark

The caddie is not decoration. He chooses a club, draws the aim line and works out the power that finishes at the hole. The red mark is that power. If he says the pin is out of reach, the mark disappears: hit a full swing down the fairway instead.

On the green he reads the slope and pace from the ball’s actual position. Follow his line and his red mark and the putt goes in far more often than a guess.

Modes

  • Stroke Play: three holes, a running card and personal bests.
  • Daily Challenge: one hole a day, the same hole, weather and wind for every copy of the game. The first finish is the score; later goes are practice. Consecutive days build a streak.
  • Match Play: hole by hole against an AI rival.
  • Skins: a pot on every hole, against AI rivals or local golfers.
  • Closest to the Pin: five shots at a tucked pin.
  • Driving Range: carry, total and offline numbers for every club.
  • Pass & Play: two to four local golfers, one ball at a time.

The two courses

Quiet Links is parkland: mown fairways, a pond, a pot bunker and a mixed wood. Dune Ridge is a coastal links: straw turf, dunes, deep rough and wind. Both are original three-hole designs; the holes are capped near 450 yards by the size of the ground.

Progress

Bests, medals and the Daily streak are kept in this browser’s local storage. There is no account and no server copy. Clearing the site data clears them. The game does not upload anything.

If something is wrong

Comfy Golf is in development. If a control does not answer, a shot looks wrong or the boards overlap on your screen, email iantrimble@remnantsecurity.com with the device and browser. The phone layout has been checked in an emulated viewport, not on every handset.
